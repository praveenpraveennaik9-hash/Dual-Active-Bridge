# Dual-Active-Bridge
Integration of Fuel cell and Battery using High Gain Interleaved Boost Converter with PMSM Drive
– Simulated an EV powertrain application in MATLAB/Simulink integration a fuel cell and battery through a high-gain
interleaved boost converter and Dual Active Bridge (DAB) for PMSM propulsion.
– Designed the power management strategy to support battery operation during fast dynamic power demand and
regenerative braking, improves overall powertrain efficiency and fuel cell utilization.
